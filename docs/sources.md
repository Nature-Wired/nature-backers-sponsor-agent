# Source References

This project uses selected public and internal reference materials to inform the Nature Backers Sponsor Agent workflow.

## Public References

### Hedera AI Agent Kit
Used for agent/plugin architecture and Hedera testnet interaction patterns.

Source: https://docs.hedera.com/hedera/open-source-solutions/ai-studio-on-hedera/hedera-ai-agent-kit

### Nature Backers Apex Prototype
Used as the prior prototype foundation for QR participation, voting, dashboard, and Hedera proof concepts.

Source: https://github.com/CarbonSustain/nature-wired-apex

### FIFA World Cup 2026 Houston Sustainability
Used as a public example of event-based sustainability positioning and local sustainability themes.

Source: https://www.fwc26houston.com/environmentalsustainability

### Green Sports Alliance Fan Engagement Playbook
Used as a public reference for sports sustainability fan engagement principles, including visible fan participation, simple calls to action, game-day activation, and measurable engagement.

Source: https://www.greensportsalliance.org/resources/playbooks

Note: The playbook is available through the Green Sports Alliance resource page and may require completing an intake/download form. The original file is not included in this public repository.

## Internal Reference Principles

The Sponsor Agent is informed by fan engagement and sponsorship strategy principles, including:

- fan participation before, during, and after events
- simple visible actions, such as QR voting or pledges
- sustainability as part of the live fan experience
- sponsor storytelling tied to participation
- measurable engagement metrics
- post-event content opportunities
- local project relevance
- digital participation for fans beyond the venue

Original internal reference files are not included in this public repo.
